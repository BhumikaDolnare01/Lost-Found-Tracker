# 🎒 Lost & Found Tracker

An AI-powered campus Lost & Found Management System designed to help students easily report, track, and recover lost items within the college campus.

## 📌 Overview

Lost & Found Tracker (LFT) is a web-based platform that simplifies the process of finding lost belongings. Students can upload details and images of lost or found items, allowing the campus community to collaboratively help reconnect owners with their belongings.

To enhance user experience, the platform also integrates an AI-powered chatbot using the Gemini API, enabling students to quickly resolve queries and navigate the system efficiently.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📸 Upload photos of lost or found items
- 🔍 Browse and search reported items
- 📋 Detailed item listings with descriptions
- 🤖 AI Chat Assistant powered by Gemini API
- 👨‍🎓 Student-friendly campus-focused interface
- 📱 Responsive and easy-to-use design
- 🔄 Real-time item tracking and status updates

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Students frequently lose personal belongings such as ID cards, wallets, keys, notebooks, and electronic devices on campus. Traditional notice boards and word-of-mouth communication are inefficient and often result in delayed recovery.

---

## 💡 Solution

Lost & Found Tracker provides a centralized digital platform where students can:

1. Report lost items with images and descriptions.
2. Post found items for identification.
3. Search existing reports to locate belongings.
4. Use an AI assistant to get instant help and guidance.
5. Improve item recovery rates through community participation.

## 📷 Use Case

A student loses their wallet on campus.

1. The student uploads a photo and description of the lost wallet.
2. Another student who finds it uploads the found item.
3. The platform helps match the reports.
4. The owner is notified and can recover their belongings quickly.

## 🌟 Impact
